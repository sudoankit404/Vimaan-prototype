FAQ

Quick answers

How is Vimaan cheaper than other ride apps?

We run a lean platform, charge riders a low flat commission and never apply surge pricing, so the savings go straight to you.

Will I always find a ride?

Yes. We keep more riders online than customers in every zone and rebalance continuously. If no rider is found, you are never charged.

What if my rider is late?

If pickup is 5+ minutes after the quoted ETA, we add ₹50 to your wallet automatically.

Who handles customer support?

Our AI assistant, available 24×7 for every kind of rider and customer issue. It resolves refunds, fare disputes, delays and more instantly.

Which payment methods are supported?

UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking, Vimaan Wallet and cash.
